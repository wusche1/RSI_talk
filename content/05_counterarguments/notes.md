The Market does not expect doom

plot: 10-year TIPS real yield, 2010 to now (data/DFII10.csv, FRED series DFII10, daily), ChatGPT release marked
- what it is: the after-inflation interest rate investors demand for lending to the US government for 10 years, i.e. the price of money now vs money in 10 years
- 2010s: between about -0.5% and 1.2%
- low: -1.19% (Aug 2021)
- 2022-2023: rise from about -1% to about 2%
- now: 2.91% (13 Nov 2026), series high 2.95% (Oct 2026)

c:
both doom and abundance should push real rates up (Chow, Halperin, Mazlish: Transformative AI, existential risk, and real interest rates)
doom: no point saving, nobody lends long without a huge yield
abundance: you will be much richer soon, so you want to borrow against it now (r ≈ ρ + γ·g, 30% growth implies double-digit real rates)
we see about 3%, not double digits, so the market expects neither
the 2022-2023 rise coincides with the Fed hiking cycle, so it cannot be attributed to AI
weak points: doom is unhedgeable (only shows up via saving decisions of the marginal investor), abundance could lower rates if gains go only to capital owners (save now to own capital), central bank and fiscal policy dominate rates
